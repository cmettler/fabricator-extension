## Kusto ADBC driver

Portions of the Substrait support (the protobuf reader, the Substrait plan
model and decoder, and test helpers) are derived from the Kusto ADBC driver
(https://github.com/CurtHagenlocher/kusto_adbc). Files derived from it retain
their original copyright headers.
